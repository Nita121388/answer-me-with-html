## Marking words is steady now, and a page folds in one click

Remark mode has a second granularity — select words inside a block instead of marking the whole block — and clicking around it
turned up six faults that could lose a mark or leave the page in a state the reader could not undo. They are fixed:

- **A mark could be deleted by tagging another phrase.** Clicking marked words reopened that mark, and the next selection in the
  same block was taken as an edit of it: tagging the new words silently replaced the old mark. The mark under a selection is now
  the one that covers exactly those words, so a new selection starts a mark of its own and the old one stays.
- **Adding a note erased the tag.** Enter in the note box saves without naming a kind, and an empty kind overwrote the one the
  mark had, so a `keep` mark came back as a plain quote. The note keeps the kind the mark already has.
- **A mark could not be clicked after the page changed.** The painted parts carried the offsets `relocate()` found rather than the
  record's own id, so after `am patch` moved the text the words were highlighted but a click found no record and Remove did
  nothing. The painted parts now carry the record id, and the record is matched to a selection by where it sits on the page.
- **Two marks that overlapped nested one `<mark>` inside another.** The inner one could not be unwrapped again, the numbers read
  `1, 3, 2`, and a reload painted a shape that could not be cleared. The ranges of a block are now worked out before the DOM
  changes, the shared words are cut away from the mark painted after them, and the numbers are written once over the marks the
  page really shows.
- **A drag over words also opened the block popover.** A drag ends with a click on the block it started in, so the block popover
  and the word float both appeared, one on top of the other. A click that comes with a live selection in its own block now picks
  words only.
- **A part that could not be wrapped lost the whole mark.** `Range.surroundContents` throws when the range crosses an element
  boundary (a formula's MathML, an inline link); the exception is caught per part, so the rest of the mark still paints.

Two more things around it:

- **Every comment and every remark in the Reply sheet has a ✕** that drops that one entry. The panel comment box empties, or the
  mark leaves the page, and the copied text follows. The sheet stays open and keeps the reader's place.
- **Copying a code block no longer copies a mark's number.** A mark inside a code block adds its note chip and its number to the
  lines; the Copy button takes both out first, so what is copied is the code.

**One button folds the whole page.** A page with foldable panels gets a toolbar button that folds every panel in one click, then
opens every panel; it reads what the page shows and its choice is remembered like a single fold's. Printing no longer stores the
print layout: the panels used to be written to storage as open while the page printed, so a reader's folds could be lost.

**Full list:** the fold button, the reply-preview ✕, and the six remark fixes above.

## 文字标注稳定了，整页也能一键折叠

标注模式除了整块标注，还能只标注块里的一段文字。在它周围点一圈，找出六个会丢标注、或把页面留在无法撤销状态的毛病，都已修好：

- **标了第二处，第一处会消失。** 点开一条已标注的文字后，同一块里接下来的选择被当成了对它的编辑：标新词时旧标注被静默替换。
  现在选中范围对应的标注，是恰好覆盖这些字的那一条，新选择会另建标注，旧的留着。
- **补批注会把类型抹掉。** 在批注框里按 Enter 保存时不指定类型，空类型覆盖了原类型，一条“保留”回来变成了普通引用。现在补批注会保留原类型。
- **页面重渲染后标注点不开。** 画到页面上的片段带的是 `relocate()` 找到的偏移，而不是记录自己的 id，`am patch` 挪动文字后字还是高亮，
  但点击找不到记录、删除也没反应。现在片段带记录 id，记录按它在页面上的位置匹配选中范围。
- **两条标注重叠时会互相嵌套 `<mark>`。** 里层再也解不开，编号变成 `1, 3, 2`，刷新后画出清不掉的结构。现在先把整块的范围算好再动 DOM，
  重叠的字从后画的那条里剪掉，编号最后按页面实际显示顺序写一遍。
- **拖选文字会同时弹出块批注弹窗。** 拖选结束时会在起始块上触发 click，块弹窗和选词浮层叠在一起。现在带选区的 click 只算选词。
- **某个片段包不上就丢掉整条标注。** `Range.surroundContents` 跨元素边界（公式的 MathML、行内链接）会抛异常；现在按片段捕获，其余部分照常画。

另外两件相关的事：

- **回复面板里每条评论、每条标注右侧都有 ✕**，可以单独去掉这一条：面板评论框清空，或标注从页面消失，被复制的文字随之更新。面板保持打开并停在原处。
- **复制代码块不会再带上标注序号。** 代码块里的标注会把自己的批注条和序号加进行内，复制按钮会先把两者去掉，复制出来的是代码本身。

**一键折叠整页。** 有可折叠面板的页面，工具栏多一个按钮：点一下全部折叠，再点一下全部展开；按钮读的是页面当前状态，选择会被记住。
打印也不再污染折叠状态：以前打印时会把面板以“展开”写进存储，读者自己的折叠可能因此丢失。
